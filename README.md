### 🧭 About me

I'm a Computing Science student at the UofA, driven by bridging the gap between theoretical models and real-world impact. I believe software should be as **maintainable** as it is **powerful** — whether I'm architecting cloud systems or fine-tuning ML pipelines, I look for the *why* behind every line of code.

- 🏗️ **Systems** — CI/CD, high-availability backends, and the infrastructure that keeps apps reliable under pressure
- 🤖 **AI in production** — moving models out of notebooks into scalable, real products (RAG, LLM tooling, audio ML)
- 🎨 **User-centric engineering** — complex data processing behind interfaces that actually feel good to use
- 🖥️ **Homelab tinkerer** — running my own Proxmox / TrueNAS / pfSense data center in my free time
